Course Description

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to computer graphics, focusing on fundamental concepts and techniques in Computer Animation and Physics Simulation. Topics include numerical integration, 3D character modeling, keyframe animation, skinning/rigging, inverse kinematics, rigid body dynamics, deformable body simulation, and fluid simulation.

Late submission policy. CS248B uses point deductions rather than "late days."

  • Late penalty. Each late day scales your score by 0.9. Work graded 85 that is two days late scores 85 × 0.92 = 68.9.
  • Maximum lateness. No assignment is accepted more than seven days after its deadline. Later submissions receive no credit.
  • Partial days round up. Any portion of a late day counts as a full day.
  • Upload grace. Submissions within 30 minutes of the deadline are not counted late. A slow upload on its own is not grounds for an extension.
  • The last submission is the one we grade. If you submit before the deadline and then resubmit afterwards, the later timestamp determines lateness for the whole assignment. If you would rather we grade your on-time version, do not resubmit.
  • Group submissions. The penalty applies to the submission, so every member of a group receives the same deduction.
  • Extensions. If illness, a family emergency, or another serious disruption affects your work, contact the instructor, before the deadline where possible and otherwise as soon as you are able. Accommodations arranged through the Office of Accessibility Education are handled separately and are not affected by this policy.
